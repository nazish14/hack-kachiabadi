# Bahawalpur Civic Infrastructure & Municipal SOPs

## Section 1: Sewerage, Drainage & Open Manholes
- **Jurisdiction & Authority**: Municipal Corporation Bahawalpur (MCB) - Water & Sanitation Branch & PHED Punjab.
- **Incident Category**: Sewerage Hazard, Missing Manhole Cover, Clogged Drain.
- **Hazard Level**: CRITICAL
- **Mandated Response Window (SLA)**: 
  - Physical Barricading & Hazard Marking: Within 2 hours.
  - Placement of Temporary Precast Concrete Cover: Maximum 4 hours.
  - Permanent Masonry Frame Repair: Maximum 24 hours.
- **Safety Protocol**: Deploy retroreflective warning cones and barrier tape immediately.
- **Required Material Manifest**: Precast RCC round cover slab (dia 21-24 inches), grade C25 concrete mix, safety barricades.

## Section 2: Solid Waste & Overflowing Dumps (Suthra Punjab)
- **Jurisdiction & Authority**: Bahawalpur Waste Management Company (BWMC) under "Suthra Punjab" Campaign.
- **Incident Category**: Roadside Garbage Pile, Dumpster Overflow, Unauthorized Dumping.
- **Hazard Level**: MODERATE TO HIGH
- **Mandated Response Window (SLA)**:
  - Commercial Corridors & Main Roads: 6 to 12 hours.
  - Residential Streets & Kachi Abadis: 12 to 24 hours.
- **Standard Operating Procedure**: Mini-dumper and loader dispatch followed by lime powder (choona) disinfection.
- **Required Material Manifest**: Compactor vehicle, lime powder bag (25kg), shovel sets, PPE gloves.

## Section 3: Road Surface, Potholes & Structural Cracks
- **Jurisdiction & Authority**: Communication & Works (C&W) Punjab for primary roads; MCB Engineering Branch for local city streets.
- **Incident Category**: Asphalt Pothole, Pavement Subsidence, Road Cut.
- **Hazard Level**: HIGH
- **Mandated Response Window (SLA)**:
  - Critical Potholes (depth > 5cm): Cold-mix temporary patch within 24 to 48 hours.
  - Minor Surface Cracks: Scheduled for bi-weekly municipal route repair.
- **Repair SOP**: Excavate loose stones, apply tack coat bitumen, infill with cold-mix asphalt, and tamp level.
- **Required Material Manifest**: Bituminous cold-mix bags (50kg), rapid-curing bitumen emulsion, manual tamper.
